Module 1

Time Series & Spectral Windows

Inspect raw spectroscopic observations across multiple indicators in stacked subpanels. Assess sampling cadence distributions and diagnose observational aliases (annual 1-yr, 6-month solar avoidance, 1-month lunar) using the NWelch spectral window function W(f).

Stacked Subpanels NWelch W(f) Alias Diagnosis Lomb-Scargle
Module 2

Welch PSD & Adaptive Segmentation

Segment datasets into continuous campaigns under 100% data preservation and zero window tapering. Overlay the Welch power spectrum on top of Lomb-Scargle periodograms on logarithmic power scales, adjust segment boundaries, and compute 1D bivariate coherence.

Zero Trimming Campaign Clustering Log Parseval PSD Bivariate Coherence
Module 3

Dual-Frequency Cross-Coherence & Slices

Quantify non-stationary stellar rotation and cross-frequency activity coupling in 2D. Explore Fisher z(f) coherence within Ramirez Delgado 2ℛ resolution cells, extract diagonal and arbitrary horizontal cuts, and run automated peak and alias identification.

2ℛ Resolution Cells Horizontal Cuts (f_rot, 2f_rot) Peak Detector Sampling Alias Beat Tags
Module 4

Multi-Campaign & Instrument Comparison

Compare spectral and coherence signatures side by side across observing seasons (such as Solar Minimum vs. Solar Maximum) or contrast different spectrographs (HARPS-N, NEID, OMNI2) with synchronized dual maps, linked horizontal slices, and delta coherence matrices.
